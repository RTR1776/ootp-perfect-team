## What this does and does not tell us

- **Round 1 of Diamond was against a "Jim" team** (an entrant who never set a roster;
  OOTP fills it with generic AI players and the game ends ~56-0). That win is not
  evidence about the build. Diamond's real result is one round.
- **Six rosters, six single-elimination runs, best-of-9.** A Bo9 between two good
  PT rosters is close to a coin flip; six of them is far too small a sample to rank
  the builds against each other or against the model. Nothing here confirms or
  refutes the run model.
- **Cap is the only berth with a live open question.** It was the most heavily worked
  of the five, and the model was found to be wrong mid-build (pitcher Control
  over-weighted against pBABIP/pHR — L.J.'s catch, confirmed on 14.9M observed BF).
  Worth checking which version of that board was actually locked in before drawing
  any conclusion from a Round 1 exit.
