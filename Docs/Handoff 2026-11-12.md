## Optimiser audit (fixed: pen ranking, the save gate, and the cap tier for item 5; the rest is open)

Verified by the audit with scratch harnesses in the session scratchpad (`exact.ts`, `toys*.ts`, `prof.ts`); re-check before changing.

1. **The batch saves rosters that fail the roster check.** Long men and backups are 25-run penalties, never legality (`roster-optimize.ts:350,376`); `current-rosters.ts:323-326` saves on the validator's "LEGAL" line alone. "2 shortstops" is off by default in both front ends (`env-roster.ts:130`, `roster-builder.tsx:492`).
2. **Position floors bind only cards moving in.** `legal()` has no position check; a start board's under-floor card stays unless replacing him gains runs, and while nobody rostered can cover him the position re-solve is off (`roster-optimize.ts:493-501`).
3. **/build searches looser floors than Claude picks:** 60 everywhere against SS 85 / CF 90 / 2B 85 / RF 75 (`event-roster-args.ts:106`).
4. **The stamina repair is post-hoc and unchecked** (`env-roster.ts:877-896`); /build has none.
5. **Under a cap the climb is not exact:** brute force on 60 random capped 13-man toys, optimum missed in 19 (mean gap 1.1 runs, max 10.1); uncapped all exact. Most misses need one upgrade paid by two downgrades; no such move exists.
6. **Pair trials skip the position re-solve** (`roster-optimize.ts:696-700`); settling each trial cut the misses 19 → 11 at twice the time.
7. **Pen candidates are ranked on raw runs**, but a pen slot pays runs less the stamina term (`roster-objective.ts:297-301` against `:251`), so a Stamina-10 reliever sits 10.8 runs low in pruning and in the downgrade frontier. Toy: 34.65 as shipped, 38.52 with the pen ranked by slot value.
8. **The dual phase is CLI-only**; /build refuses to Optimise a loaded dual pick.
9. **`orderStaff` runs after the dual decision** and ignores `dualCorrection` (toy 91.6 → 85.8).
10. **Optimise is the weakest path:** no staff tier, 7 starts, a 45 s wall-clock cut, so results vary with machine load.
11. **Staff caps (5/7/12) are not in the search**, only in the baseline shape.
12. **Speed:** one capped 26-man climb is 24.8 s and 477k whole-board evaluations, ~18 s of it pairs; `legal` 21%, the objective 22%, `missingBackups` 13%. Cuts: a cap-delta check before `place`, delta scoring, pricing pairs from their parts.
13. **Tests missing:** a brute-force small-instance check of the whole climb; an invariant fuzz (every legal result passes the validator, floors and audit); the stamina repair and dual phase.
14. **An integer program is realistic** for this objective; an LP bound alone would give every climb an optimality gap.
