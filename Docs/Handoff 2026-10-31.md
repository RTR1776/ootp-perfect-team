## Builder changes (all in lib/ or env-roster; PR #157 put the lib changes live)

- **Two-way cards in both roles** (L.J.: "Rile is literally the meta card … because of two way"). PTCS 7 Championship Open: 35 of the 36 teams carrying Ed Rile used him at 1B *and* in the rotation.
  - `two-way.ts` `unitOf` / `dualUseOk`: a card and its other-role form are ONE roster unit (size, value, variants, tier slots, same-player). A starter may also hold a lineup or bench spot; a reliever only a bench spot.
  - `roster-optimize` `legal` and `roster-rules` `validateRoster` count units; the validator allows the pairing (so /build shows a dual roster as legal).
  - `roster-objective` `dualCorrection`: a starter who is also in the lineup cedes his starts' share of that spot to the best bench card (his bat on those days is `starterBat`, no DH).
  - env-roster **dual phase** after the normal climb: one extra bench slot (bats + 1), seeded with each of the best 4 owned two-way cards in both roles (the arm in the weakest SP slot; when the cap or tier slots overflow, the dearest bench bats are traded down), climbed, kept when it beats the normal board. `--no-dual`, `--dual-try N`.
  - Results: Championship Cap 332.3 → 337.8 (Rile SP1 + 1B, saved 1609); Early Years Cap 213.8 → 224.3 (Luther Farrell SP4 + bench bat, 1621); Championship Open +0.3 only (elite 1B owned).
- **Backups in cap and slots events need a 60 glove, not the starter's floor** (`CAP_BACKUP_FLOOR`, `backupFloorAt`). Measured over 1,770 teams in 11 cap series (`Tourney Data/Cap Exports`): the best quarter carry two catchers (74%), an IF utility and an OF utility man more often than the rest, each at a median +1 over the floor value. Two catchers stay required. (A "waive backups without a floor card" rule was tried first and reverted.)
- **Cap ledger** (`lib/cap-ledger.ts`, printed by env-roster and `--score` in cap events): per card, value over the floor, runs over the best unrostered floor card in his slots, runs per cap point, the rule he is there for (backup, 2nd C); leaks flagged.
- **Field average** (env-roster, display only): the usage-weighted average card actually played in the series (observed_card_stats), scored the same way; each roster row shows `fld` = runs over it. The runs columns themselves are against the ERA's league-average line, not the field (L.J. asked). Late Silver's field: bat −5.9 vs RHP / −8.8 vs LHP, arm −1.3.
- Gotchas found: `env-roster --ban/--must` take card NAMES, not ids. `pkill -f <pattern>` kills the calling shell when the pattern is in its own command line — kill by PID. The container restarted once mid-batch (killed a nohup batch); use the resumable runner pattern below.
