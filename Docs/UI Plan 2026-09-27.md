## 1. Goal

Keep every number the app computes, but have each page answer its one question on the first screen, with method notes and long tables one click away. Make every change undoable. Show every event rule wherever cards are recommended, and flag a missing rule from the field's own play, so a roster can't become illegal or get lost without the screen saying so.

## 2. Principles

| # | Rule | Built with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Answer first; explain on request.** A one-line summary (16 words or fewer) that names the decision. No more than 25 words of visible prose before the first table or chart. ### Decisions this plan makes where reviewers disagreed

- **Missing card-set rules are found from data first.** PR 0 proposes the missing rules from what each event's field actually plays. Where there is no play on file, L.J. confirms the rule by name. The UI then flags any rule that is still missing, using the same evidence.
- **Card links** go to a new single-card mode, `/cards?id=<cardId>&event=<id>`, not `?q=<name>`. A name can match several cards (two Hank Aarons, two Piazzas). The mode ships in PR 9 together with CardName.
- **One card-set map.** `CARD_TYPE_NAME` + `CARD_TYPE_SHORT` live in `roster-rules.ts`. Reviewers also called it `CARD_TYPE_LABEL`; use the one name.
- **One event scorer.** `event-score.ts` serves Build, Draft, Cards and Market, so a card's Runs can't differ between pages again.
- **Table headers** are `text-2xs font-medium` and not uppercase, so pWOBA, pFIP and wOBA* read correctly. This follows build-8 over SH-8.
- **The two environment pages stay two routes**, renamed **Event read** (/runenv) and **Compare events** (/environments). They share a picker and a tab switcher [env-m4]. Merging them into one page (SH-12) comes later.
- **Card Model undo** is one history stack, a toast, and inline confirms. There is no persisted "Removed:" row and no per-field ↺. Each field shows its base value as a caption instead [cardmodel-2 check, cardmodel-m3].
- **PR 4 ships in two parts.** 4a is the undo and jank fix L.J. asked for; 4b holds the lock menus, the tables, the form and the copy.
- **The Build picker's "Specials" group does not move to the top.** 8 of its 14 rows are finished PTCS 6 events. Recent events plus search fix the ordering [home-4 check].
- **PTCS Team results stay on /ptcs**, in a collapsed History section filtered to the period. They do not move to /played [ptcs-2 check].
- **Data freshness.** Generic freshness (collection, shop list, league, dump, play) moves to one sidebar `FreshnessCard`. Page-specific dates stay: PTCS line and cwhit ages, Market's ownership date, and Build's collection line, which now appears only when the collection is stale.
- **Arms in the Card Model ship in two phases.** Phase 1 (PR 5) shows the pitching staff and scores arms from observed league play. Arms with little or no league sample get a ratings estimate from one fitted slope (`k_arm`), labelled "estimate". Without the estimate, most shop arms would show nothing. Phase 2 replaces the slope with a full arm fit (§5 F).

## 5. Card Model: pitchers and undo

### A. What the data supports

- **Stats.** `league_stints` pitcher rows have role (SP/RP/CL), IP, BF, G, GS, K, BB, HP, HR, ER, R, SIERA and WAR.
- **Ratings.** STU, CON, PBAB, HRA and STM, plus split STU/CON/PBAB/HRA vL/vR since 2026-09-26 (`ingest/league.ts:140-201`). Pitcher vL/vR snapshots are vs left- and right-handed batters.
- **No arm model yet.** There is no ratings fit for arms; the League Model doc says "the fit is bats only so far". /league's pitcher board pools raw FIP/FIP* across leagues and is not league-relative. The HD plan measures a pitcher by his pooled FIP-component edge against each week's own league, and the app uses the same measure.
- **Sample coverage.** The /league?week=all payload has 315 pooled pitcher cards: 137 with at least 500 IP and 97 with at least 1,000 IP. Played lists 1,836 arms. Observed edge alone would leave most shop arms unscored, along with every variant no league team has rostered (the Nen/Gossage case in cardmodel-4). That's why Phase 1 includes the `k_arm` estimate.
- **Movement** isn't in the export and isn't modelled (`card-value.ts:187`); pHR and pBABIP carry it.
- **Today pitchers are filtered out** at `page.tsx:22`, at `route.ts:82/130` ("No hitter with that card id.") and at `league-hitters.ts:106`.

### B. Phase 1 data layer: `web/src/lib/league-arms.ts` (PR 5)

1. **`myLeagueArms()`**, the pitcher mirror of `myLeagueBats` (`league-hitters.ts:98-108`). Returns `{league, on, arms: {entry: "Name#cid", role, gs, ip, stamina}[]}`, with role SP when GS > 0.
2. **`armEdges({family?: LeagueFamily|'all', split: 'all'|'vL'|'vR'}) → Map<key, ArmEdge>`.** The key is the `hitKey` from `league-board.ts` (cid, plus "v" for variants).
   - For each complete snapshot s (MIN_PITCHER_SHARE rule), from rostered non-free-agent pitchers: `lgCore_s = (13·ΣHR + 3·(ΣBB+ΣHP) − 2·ΣK) / ΣIP`.
   - For each stint: `core_i = (13·HR + 3·(BB+HP) − 2·K) / IP_i`. The FIP constant cancels.
   - `edge9 = Σ IP_i·(lgCore_s − core_i) / Σ IP_i`. Positive means better than that week's league.
   - Compute it separately for SP stints (`asSP`) and for RP/CL stints (`asRP`), each as `{edge9, ip, teams, weeks}`.
   - Add `edge9Reg = edge9 · ip / (ip + 150)`, the same 150 IP prior as `REG.ip`.
   - vL/vR: the same computation over the split snapshots. `lhbShare[family]` = ΣBF(vL)/ΣBF(all) for the newest ordinary week.
3. **`ipPerSlot(family)`**, from the newest complete week.
   - SP: the median per-team IP of each team's 5 arms with the most GS.
   - RP: the mean IP of the other arms with at least 10 IP.
   - Season runs = `edge9 × ipPerSlot / 9`.
   - Sanity check against the HD plan: Jansen vs Henke/Blue gives (0.29 + 0.05) × 70 / 9 ≈ 2.6, against the plan's "+2.7 per 70 IP".
   - Fall back to 180 and 70 IP when the week is incomplete.
4. **`staffSolve(arms, locks)`.**
   - Rotation: the 5 best item-5 scores as SP among arms with Stamina > 25 (the `roleRuns` break in `card-value.ts`).
   - Bullpen: the rest, by their item-5 score as RP.
   - Locks pin an entry to SP1–SP5, CL or RP1–RPn.
   - Total = Σ score × ipPerSlot(role) / 9.
   - `addArm(candidate) = staffSolve(with) − staffSolve(without)`, the same pattern as `add()` in `league-lineup.ts`.
   - Leverage isn't modelled, and the footer says so.
5. **Scores and the `k_arm` estimate** (moved from Phase 2).
   - `appPer9(face)`: from `envFitMaps`, the arm's runs saved per 700 BF on each side (same era and park as `leagueLineups`), converted to per 9 IP using the week's BF/IP and weighted by `lhbShare`.
   - Fit `k_arm` once with `web/scripts/fit-arm-slope.ts`. It is the slope of observed `edge9` on `appPer9` over the 97 cards with at least 1,000 league IP. Store it as `arms.shrink` in `league-model.json`.
   - With at least 150 league IP: `edge9Reg + k_arm·(appPer9(typed) − appPer9(base))`. That is just `edge9Reg` when nothing is typed. Source `'league'`.
   - With under 150 IP, or none: `k_arm·(appPer9(typed) − appPer9_lg)`. Source `'estimate'`, labelled "estimate from ratings; no league sample".

### C. API: `/api/league-card` (PR 5)

- **GET `?card=id`** allows pitchers. It returns:
  - `kind: 'arm'`;
  - the face `{STU vL, STU vR, CON vL, CON vR, HRA vL, HRA vR, PBABIP vL, PBABIP vR, STM}`;
  - movement `{vL, vR}` (read-only);
  - observed `{asSP, asRP, vL, vR}`.
- **POST** adds `arms?: string[]` (default `myLeagueArms`), `armLocks?: Record<slot, entry>`, candidate `kind: 'bat'|'arm'`, and the candidate's typed arm face.
- **The response adds:**
  - `staff: {rotation: ArmSlot[5], bullpen: ArmSlot[], total, ipPerSlot, lhbShare}`
  - `ArmSlot = {slot, entry, label, role, edge9, edge9vL, edge9vR, sampleIp, source: 'league'|'estimate', runs}`
  - `armPool[] = {entry, label, asSP, asRP, stamina}`
  - `candidateArm = {label, edge9 by role and side, source}`
  - `armAdd = {season, wins, replaces}`
