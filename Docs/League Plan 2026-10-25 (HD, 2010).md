### What defense is worth in league play (new, `scripts/defense-study/league-def.mts`)

**Method:** team runs allowed per 1,400 IP within each league-week, with pitching held by its outcomes (HR, BB+HBP, K). 1,641 team-weeks (1,005 with all eight positions rated), 53 league-weeks.

| | CF | SS | RF | 1B | 3B | LF | 2B | C |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Measured | **+5.9 ± 1.9** | +2.7 ± 1.3 | +1.6 ± 0.5 | +1.4 ± 0.8 | +0.6 ± 0.5 | +0.4 ± 0.5 | +0.3 ± 1.2 | −0.9 ± 1.2 |

- Center field is the one glove that matters in league, at about 6× the model's price. Even the low end of its range is 2×.
