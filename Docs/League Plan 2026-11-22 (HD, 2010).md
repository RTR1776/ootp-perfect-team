## 8. Follow-ups (L.J., 11-22)

**Is an 8th reliever needed? No.**

Every full PEL/HD/LD team-season since 09-20: 939 HD/LD teams, 318 PEL.

- **In HD, pen size doesn't separate good teams from bad.** Relievers with 20+ IP: top quarter by win% 7.1, bottom quarter 7.2. Arms used over a season: 13.6 in every quarter.
- **The 8th reliever barely pitches.** Only 116 of 252 top-quarter HD teams used one at all, and he threw 24 innings.
- **Relief innings by rank (top quarter):**

  | Rank | Relief IP | Typical stamina |
  |---|---|---|
  | #1 | 90 | 60–70 |
  | #2 | 73 | 60–70 |
  | #3 | 64 | 60–70 |
  | #4 | 58 | ~40 |
  | #5 | 51 | ~40 |
  | #6 | 44 | ~40 |
  | #7 | 35 | ~40 |

- **An 8th arm costs a bat, and every one of the 14 bats has a platoon role.** The cheapest bat to cut is worth about 4 runs a season (Reggie 3.8, Seager 4.2). An 8th arm's ~24 innings are worth about 1.
- **PEL is different:** its top quarter used 8.0 relievers with 20+ IP against 7.0–7.3 for the rest. Not the question for HD.

**Yamamoto against Plank and Hershiser, dug further.**

Raw SIERA, ERA and K/9 favour Yamamoto (3.98 / 4.17 / 7.33). Against each one's own league average, since 09-01:

| | SIERA | ERA | K/9 | League average SIERA |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Hershiser | +0.01 | −0.09 | −0.39 | 4.20 |
| Plank | +0.12 | −0.09 | −0.56 | 4.17 |

- **Half his raw edge is context.** His leagues ran lower (4.02 SIERA against 4.17–4.20).
- **Against his own leagues he's still about 0.15 per 9 better on SIERA,** but no better on runs allowed.
- **The difference is home runs, which SIERA ignores and OOTP rates (pHR).**

  | HR per 100 BF | vs LHB | vs RHB | pHR rating |

  - Plank (LHP) faces 72% RHB, so it cuts his.

- **Verdict:** Plank SP5 and Hershiser long man, by about 3 runs. Yamamoto stays in reserve.
