**Still to fill:** the last lineup spot (C or 1B), a backup C, 4 SP, 4 RP and 5 bench.

- **Starters come first among the arms.** A Bronze SP offers about +11 a pack, a Bronze RP +4. The best Bronze starters are Mickey Lolich, Bill Duggleby and Al Spalding.
- **Catchers:** Ernie Whitt 68, James McCann 63 (new 11-17) and Jason Castro 68 are the best Bronze C. You need two.
- **The bench and the open spot:** take gloves, at SS (L.J.'s two-shortstop rule) and 2B/3B.
- **Relievers last.** The best Bronze RP adds about 8 runs.

**How to read the numbers.** Every score is in runs over a replacement card at that spot, in this event's run environment and park, with gloves and catcher framing in. It is the live helper's scale, the same as Build's.
- **What a pack offers** is the expected best card at a spot among the cards of that spot a 12-card mixed pack shows. That is about one card at each bat spot and 2–4 starters a pack.
- **Gain** is what a card adds to an empty team at its best spot: the helper's quick read. In the draft the helper re-ranks every pack against the team you have, with the rounds left priced in, so trust it over these lists.

**Assumed:** 128 teams and mixed positions in each pack. There are no exports of this event, so the field's handedness is the default (30% of the pitching left-handed). The tables include the cards released on 11-17 where the card year fits, marked "new". New cards can turn up in drafts (L.J., 11-17), and the live helper already has them in its pools.
