## The finding worth acting on

`pnpm era:slopes` regresses a card's observed wOBA deviation from its own
series mean against the ratings on its face, per era band, PA-weighted —
6,666 card-series rows over 10.9M PA. **The rating→outcome relationship is
not one curve with an era term on top.**

Runs per 700 PA per +10 rating:

| era | Power | BABIP |
|---|---|---|
| Deadball ≤1920 | **0.17** | **2.88** |
| Free Agency 1977-93 | 1.78 | 2.66 |
| Steroid 1994-2009 | **1.87** | **0.53** |
| Modern 2010+ | 1.67 | 1.16 |

In deadball Power is statistically indistinguishable from zero
(0.30 ± 0.22 wOBA points) while BABIP is 5.14 ± 0.29 — seventeen times the
value. In the steroid era it inverts. Not a range artifact: Power SD is
32-34 in every band.

Against the shipped curves (the printed sensitivity line is PRE-calibration,
so ×0.5114 to compare): at 1977-93 the model prices Power 1.99 vs 1.78
observed — fine — but **BABIP 1.07 vs 2.66 observed**, under-priced ~2.5×,
and worse the further back you go. Gap is under-priced too; Eye is
over-priced nearly everywhere and is zero in modern.

**Caveat before anyone refits on this:** those standard errors are
card-level and therefore optimistic. Cards inside one series share a park, a
field and an opponent pool, so the honest unit is the series, and some bands
rest on **four** of them (Deadball 4, Live Ball 3). Effect sizes are far too
large to be noise; the precision is not what the ± suggests. More series per
band is what the upload push buys. Per-era curve refit is the real next
project, not a tweak.

## Traps found today (all now in RUNBOOK.md)

- **A hand-swapped export is not re-imported.** `import:observed` hashes
  content and will pick up a changed file, but only when something runs it.
  A 2-team sliver of `diamondvariety_26` sat live in the DB for an hour
  after the full 121-team export replaced it on disk. Run
  `pnpm import:observed --series <slug>` after any manual swap.
- **Check team counts on a filing push.** An export taken from the wrong
  screen carries only your own matchup: ~50 rows and 2 teams against ~3,000
  and 120+. It imports clean and quietly dilutes the series average. Three
  slipped in today and are quarantined in `Archive/Truncated/`.
- **Never read a card's KIND off its title.** A Snapshot is titled after its
  SET, so a legal field shows cards reading "Baseball Reference", "World
  Baseball Classic" and "Super Utility" that are all `card_type 7`. Use
  `card_type`. Verified: all 247 cards in the run-26 Diamond Variety export
  are type 2, 6 or 7 and nothing else. (An earlier note on that catalogue
  row claiming the rule rotates weekly came from this misreading and has
  been withdrawn.)
- **Two filers at once will stack dialogs.** Launch it once and leave it
  running; the report now self-dismisses after 90s either way.
