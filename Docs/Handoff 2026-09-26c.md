## League card model (#38)

L.J. asked for a way to model a card for league by typing its numbers, for shop variants before buying. **/league-card** (nav: League → Card Model):

- **Form:** pick the base card and its ratings prefill. Type over them with the card face's vL/vR Eye, Power, Gap, BABIP, Avoid K and the positions. A blank position means the card cannot play there. "Apply step" multiplies the base by the typical variant step (7.5% by default) for a side the face doesn't show. League (PEL/HD/LD), run environment and glove weight (full / half / bat only) are selectable.
- **Scoring:** `GET/POST /api/league-card`. The team is L.J.'s bats in the newest league export (`myLeagueBats`), and the list is editable.
- **Shared code:** the scoring moved into `lib/analytics/league-lineup.ts` and roster resolution into `lib/league-hitters.ts`. `league:compare` now uses both, and its output is byte-identical to before in three setups (HD; PEL 1989 with gloves ×0.5; LD with no DH).
