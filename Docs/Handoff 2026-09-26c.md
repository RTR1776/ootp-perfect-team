## Model, in one paragraph

Bats rank at r 0.69 within a field against how they played (0.73 with observed play blended), well calibrated, and near what this data supports. None of these beat the shipped model: refitting curves, per-event environments, a PT-calibrated era table, per-band recalibration, or similarity-weighted blending. The Review has the numbers.

Weak spots:
- Iron/Bronze fields (r 0.41)
- arms (0.52 vs FIP, 0.42 vs runs allowed)
- relievers (0.29)

The biggest remaining lever is data: record the ratings each copy played with (variant level, Live rating updates). The export rows carry them and the import drops them. Spec in the Review, next step 1.
