- **Low Bronze (rounds 10–12).** 3B (+9.7) and SS (+12.8) are thin here, so have both filled before round 10. Bronze 1B, LF and RF are fine (+17 to +18).
- **Low Iron (round 13).** Bench only. Take the backup C (Schrecongost) or the second SS (Sam Bankhead), whichever you're missing; L.J.'s rule is two of each.

- **What a pack offers** is the expected best card at a spot among the cards of that spot a 12-card mixed pack shows. That is about one card at each bat spot and 2–4 starters a pack.
- **Gain** is what a card adds to an empty team at its best spot: the helper's quick read. In the draft the helper re-ranks every pack against the team you have, with the rounds left priced in, so trust it over these lists.

**Assumed:** 128 teams and mixed positions in each pack. There are no exports of this event, so the field's handedness is the default (30% of the pitching left-handed).
