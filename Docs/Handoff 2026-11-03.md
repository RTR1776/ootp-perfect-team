## Friends' engine reference (`~/Downloads/OOTP_Engine_Decision_Reference.pdf`)
- **Two different numbers:**
  - *Outing capacity:* past it, effectiveness drops in that outing. Walks jump right away and home runs rise.
  - *Recovery ceiling:* the workload he can recover from by his next start.
- Both depend on the era band; their tool's 1959 figures don't carry to other eras.
- **Relievers:** below 35% readiness they're penalised from the first pitch.
- **Pen selection:** the AI weights a reliever's split against the next 3 hitters at 40/65/90% for L/R slider −5/0/+5.
- **Open:** the 1919 capacity numbers. Ask the friends to run their tool at 1919, stamina 80 and 100. Our 150–170 pitch advice for deadball 90+ stamina may be past capacity.
